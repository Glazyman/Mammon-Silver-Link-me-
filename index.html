About Us

Four Generations of Silver

Mammon Silver is a fourth-generation family business specializing in estate silver and antiques — built on generations of experience, trust, and expertise.

Our family's roots trace back to the historic London Silver Vaults, where generations of dealers developed a deep understanding of estate silver and fine antiques. In the 1950s, Joseph Mammon continued that tradition, building a reputation for honesty and expertise. His son, Laurence Mammon, carried the family business forward and expanded its experience in estate silver and antiques.

Today, Nathaniel Mammon proudly represents the fourth generation of the family business, combining traditional knowledge with a modern understanding of today's silver market.

We specialize in American, English, and European silver, purchasing everything from a single piece to complete estate collections. Our goal is to provide fair evaluations, transparent service, and a straightforward, professional experience.

Built on generations of experience, Mammon Silver continues a family tradition rooted in the world of silver and antiques.
